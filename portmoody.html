City of Port Moody · Council term 2022–2026

What Council decided and how each member voted

Independent summary, not affiliated with the City of Port Moody. Every recorded Council vote from November 2022 to July 2026, read from the City of Port Moody's council minutes (108 sets), with the motion text, the result and each member's vote. Port Moody's minutes record motions as “Moved, seconded, and CARRIED” without naming the mover, so movers are not shown here. Routine motions such as adopting the agenda or adjourning are left out.

Compiled from public council records. This page may contain transcription or processing errors; the linked official records are authoritative. Spotted a mistake? Email georgesteinberg15@gmail.com.

About NO votes

A NO vote applies to the specific motion shown. It does not necessarily mean the councillor opposed the underlying project or issue. Council frequently votes on amendments, referrals, timing changes and procedural motions before voting on the final proposal. Read the motion text before interpreting a NO vote.

About this data

Interpreting voting records

Voting records show how members voted on individual motions, but they do not by themselves show who developed or introduced an initiative. This source does not name who moved or seconded each motion, so that part of each member’s record is not shown here.

A vote against an amendment, referral or procedural motion should also not automatically be interpreted as opposition to the underlying issue.

The site presents the motion text and official minutes so users can review the context themselves. It does not rate members or describe them as supporters or opponents of an issue.

Source

The City of Port Moody's Regular and Special Council minutes, 1 November 2022 to 28 July 2026 (108 sets), retrieved 6 October 2026 from the City's meetings portal. Port Moody publishes its minutes as attachments to the agenda of the later meeting that adopts them, so the copies used here are the versions presented for adoption, most of them marked “draft”. Council occasionally corrects minutes before adopting them. Each vote links to its minutes.

The official record is authoritative. Where this page and the official record differ, the official record is correct.

How votes were counted

Port Moody records each result as “CARRIED” or “DEFEATED”, followed by “Voting against” and the names of those opposed. Members named there are counted against; everyone else present is counted in favour. Attendance comes from each meeting's present and absent lists, adjusted where the minutes note that a member entered, left, returned or declared a conflict of interest.

Mover and seconder

Port Moody's minutes say “Moved, seconded, and CARRIED” without names, so every vote on this page says “Mover not available from source”. The mover is never inferred.

Limits

  • Minutes could not be found for nine meetings, so their votes are not included: the Regular Council meetings of 12 and 26 November 2024, Special Council meetings on 6 June 2023, 15 and 25 October, 5 November and 3 December 2024, and 7 July 2026, and the 29 September 2026 meeting, whose minutes had not yet been presented.
  • Closed-meeting resolutions (“Special Council to Close”), Public Hearings and committee meetings are not included.
  • Items adopted on the consent agenda were approved together in one vote; they are listed separately and marked.
  • Where a member arrived late or left early and the minutes give only a time, not the point in the meeting, they are counted as present.
  • The minutes are PDFs whose text layer sometimes puts stray spaces inside words; these appear in some motion text here. About 3% of result lines could not be tied to a motion and are left out.
  • Each reading of a bylaw is its own vote.

Types of motion

Each vote is labelled Main motion, Amendment, Referral, Postponement, Reconsideration, Procedural or Other, based on how the minutes describe it and on the wording of the motion. “Procedural” here mostly means receiving a report for information. The labels are assigned by this site and are a guide; the motion text is shown in full.

How this page was made

The data was compiled with AI assistance. Software read each set of minutes and extracted the votes, motion text, movers and seconders; the results were checked against samples of the originals, not vote by vote. This page may therefore contain transcription or processing errors.
