# North Shore Council Votes

How each member of the three North Shore councils voted during the 2022–2026 term: per-member records, voting blocs, votes by category, and a searchable log of every recorded vote.

- `index.html`: home page comparing all three councils
- `district.html`: District of North Vancouver, from the District's [Council voting decisions](https://www.dnv.org/page/council-voting-decisions) register
- `city.html`: City of North Vancouver, rebuilt from the City's [Council meeting minutes](https://www.cnv.org/City-Hall/Council-Meetings/Council-Agendas,-Minutes-and-Videos)
- `west.html`: District of West Vancouver, rebuilt from its [Council meeting minutes](https://westvancouver.ca/mayor-council/meetings/past)

Independent summary, not affiliated with any municipality. Every vote links to the official minutes. Names of private residents and individual applicants are removed from motion text.
