# DNV Council Voting Record

How each member of the District of North Vancouver Council voted, November 2022 to July 2026: per-member records, voting blocs, votes by category, and a searchable log of every recorded vote.

Independent summary, not affiliated with the District of North Vancouver.

**Source:** the District's [Council voting decisions](https://www.dnv.org/page/council-voting-decisions) register. Every vote links to the official minutes and meeting video. Names of private residents are removed from motion text.
