Corrections and changes

What has been corrected or added on this site, newest first. Site last updated October 10, 2026.

Compiled from public council records. The pages may contain transcription or processing errors; the linked official records are authoritative. Spotted a mistake? Email georgesteinberg15@gmail.com with the council, date and item. Every report is checked against the official record, and corrections are listed here.

October 10, 2026 · AdditionAll councils

Added a link for each vote and each councillor’s record, vote numbering under an agenda heading on every page, a search across all councils, spreadsheet downloads, a guide to reading a vote and this log.

October 6, 2026 · AdditionCity of Vancouver

Added motion text, movers and seconders from the official minutes. Where the City’s voting data and the minutes differ, the difference is shown on the vote.

October 4, 2026 · AdditionDistrict of North Vancouver

Votes on the same agenda item are now numbered in the order recorded in the minutes.

October 3, 2026 · CorrectionDistrict of North Vancouver

Restored the November 14, 2023 referral vote on the Deep Cove Parking Review, which had been left out in error. The District’s voting register and the official minutes describe it differently; the vote is shown with that discrepancy noted.

October 3, 2026 · CorrectionDistrict of North Vancouver

Corrected nine votes where the District’s voting register differed from the official minutes. The site now follows the minutes and shows each difference on the vote under “Source discrepancy”.

October 3, 2026 · CorrectionDistrict of North Vancouver

Corrected a summary about parks votes and two other summary statements that did not accurately describe individual members’ votes.

October 3, 2026 · CorrectionAll councils

Removed summaries written by this site that characterized members or groups of members. Pages now show the motion text, the mover and seconder where the minutes name them, the result and each member’s vote, without ratings or labels.

October 3, 2026 · CorrectionSurrey, Burnaby, New Westminster, West Vancouver

Some members who had left the room for an item, for example after declaring a conflict of interest, had been shown as voting in favour. They are now shown as absent for those votes.

October 3, 2026 · CorrectionSurrey, New Westminster

Some votes against that the minutes record in a different format had been missed. They are now shown.

October 3, 2026 · CorrectionBurnaby

Minutes with rotated pages had been misread by the software. Those meetings were read again and their votes restored.

October 3, 2026 · CorrectionAll councils

Fixed a display fault that showed all four sections of a page at once instead of one tab at a time.

October 3, 2026 · AdditionAll councils

Added a corrections contact and a notice on every page that the data may contain transcription or processing errors.
