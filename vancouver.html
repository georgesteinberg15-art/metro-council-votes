City of Vancouver · Council term 2022–2026

What Council decided and how each member voted

Independent summary, not affiliated with the City of Vancouver. Every recorded Council vote from November 2022 to August 2026, from the City of Vancouver's official council voting records on its open data portal, with the agenda item, the result and each member's vote. That dataset does not include the wording of each motion or who moved it, so those are not shown here; each vote links to its meeting page. Thirteen people served on Council this term: Christine Boyle and Adriane Carr left, and Lucy Maloney and Sean Orr joined after the April 2025 by-election.

About NO votes

A NO vote applies to the specific motion shown. It does not necessarily mean the councillor opposed the underlying project or issue. Council frequently votes on amendments, referrals, timing changes and procedural motions before voting on the final proposal. Read the motion text before interpreting a NO vote. On this page the City's data gives the agenda item but not the wording of the motion, and several votes on one item (amendments, then the main motion) can share a title. About this data

Interpreting voting records

Voting records show how members voted on individual motions, but they do not by themselves show who developed or introduced an initiative. This source does not name who moved or seconded each motion, so that part of each member’s record is not shown here.

A vote against an amendment, referral or procedural motion should also not automatically be interpreted as opposition to the underlying issue.

The site presents the agenda item and a link to the official meeting page so users can review the context themselves. It does not rate members or describe them as supporters or opponents of an issue.

Source

The City of Vancouver's Council voting records dataset, retrieved 3 October 2026. It covers Council, standing committee, public hearing, special Council and Auditor General Committee votes from 7 November 2022 to 14 August 2026, with each member's vote recorded by the City. Each vote links to its meeting page, which has the agenda, minutes and video.

The official record is authoritative. Where this page and the official record differ, the official record is correct.

How votes were counted

The City records each member as in favour, in opposition, absent, abstaining, declaring a conflict, or not voting. Abstentions and conflicts are shown here as no recorded vote and listed on the vote; under BC law an abstention is counted as a vote in favour.

Mover and seconder

The City's voting dataset does not record the mover or seconder, so every vote on this page says “Mover not available from source”. The official minutes do name them; they are linked from each meeting page. The mover is never inferred.

Limits

  • The dataset gives the agenda item and outcome but not the wording of each motion.
  • The type of motion is shown only where the City's own title states it (for example “Amendment” or “Referral”). Otherwise it is shown as not stated.

Types of motion

The City’s data does not classify motions. A type is shown only where the City’s own title for the vote states it; otherwise it is shown as not stated.

How this page was made

The data was compiled with AI assistance. Software read the City’s dataset and extracted the votes; the results were checked against samples of the originals, not vote by vote. This page may therefore contain transcription or processing errors.
