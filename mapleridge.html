What Council decided, who moved it and how each member voted

Independent summary, not affiliated with the City of Maple Ridge. Every recorded Council vote from November 2022 to September 2026, read from the City of Maple Ridge's official meeting minutes (105 sets), with the motion text, the result and each member's vote. The mover and seconder are shown from August 2024, when Maple Ridge's minutes began naming them. Routine motions such as approving the agenda or adjourning are left out.

Compiled from public council records. This page may contain transcription or processing errors; the linked official records are authoritative. Spotted a mistake? Email georgesteinberg15@gmail.com.

About NO votes

A NO vote applies to the specific motion shown. It does not necessarily mean the councillor opposed the underlying project or issue. Council frequently votes on amendments, referrals, timing changes and procedural motions before voting on the final proposal. Read the motion text before interpreting a NO vote.

About this data

Interpreting voting records

Voting records show how members voted on individual motions, but they do not by themselves show who developed or introduced an initiative. Where the minutes name them, this site therefore also identifies the councillor who moved and seconded each motion.

A vote against an amendment, referral or procedural motion should also not automatically be interpreted as opposition to the underlying issue.

The site presents the motion text and official minutes so users can review the context themselves. It does not rate members or describe them as supporters or opponents of an issue.

Source

The City of Maple Ridge's official minutes on its meetings portal: 105 sets of Regular and Special Council minutes from 1 November 2022 to 8 September 2026, retrieved 7 October 2026. Each vote links to its minutes and, where available, the meeting video.

The official record is authoritative. Where this page and the official record differ, the official record is correct.

How votes were counted

Maple Ridge records each result as “CARRIED” or “DEFEATED”. Recent minutes list those opposed, absent or in conflict by name before the result; earlier minutes add “with Councillor … opposed” after it. Members named as opposed are counted against; everyone else present is counted in favour. Attendance comes from each meeting's present and absent lists and those per-vote notes, adjusted where the minutes note that a member left or returned.

Mover and seconder

From August 2024 the minutes introduce each motion with “Moved by: Councillor …, Seconded by: Councillor …”, and those names are shown exactly as recorded. Earlier minutes say only “Moved and seconded”, so earlier votes say “Mover not available from source”. The mover is never inferred.

Limits

  • Six sets of minutes are scanned images that could not be read, so their votes are not included: 17 and 31 January, 14 February, 21 and 28 November and 5 December 2023. No minutes were posted for four other meetings (11 April 2023, 17 December 2024, 15 December 2025 and 29 September 2026).
  • The minutes changed layout three times during the term. About 5% of result lines could not be tied to a motion and are left out.
  • Some minutes are PDFs whose text layer puts stray spaces inside words; these appear in some motion text here.
  • Committee of the Whole, Council Workshop, Public Hearing and closed meetings are not included. Each reading of a bylaw is its own vote.

Types of motion

Each vote is labelled Main motion, Amendment, Referral, Postponement, Reconsideration, Procedural or Other, based on how the minutes describe it and on the wording of the motion. “Procedural” here mostly means receiving a report for information. The labels are assigned by this site and are a guide; the motion text is shown in full.

How this page was made

The data was compiled with AI assistance. Software read each set of minutes and extracted the votes, motion text, movers and seconders; the results were checked against samples of the originals, not vote by vote. This page may therefore contain transcription or processing errors.
